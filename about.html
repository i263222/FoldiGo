OUR ECO-MISSION

Every year, billions of single-use plastic bags end up in landfills. FoldiGo was designed to solve plastic waste and forgotten shopping bags in one portable package.

Why Choose FoldiGo?

  • Always On You: Clips directly to your keys or backpack so you never forget your bag again.
  • Reusable & Durable: Made from high-strength ripstop polyester.
  • Eco-Friendly: One FoldiGo eliminates over 500 plastic bags per year.
