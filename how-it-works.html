HOW IT WORKS

Follow these simple steps to fold and unfold your bag in seconds.

4 Steps to Fold:

  1. Empty out your bag after shopping.
  2. Flatten the bag and fold the left and right sides inward.
  3. Roll the bag tightly from top to bottom toward the pouch.
  4. Tuck it inside the attached silicone shell and zip/snap into keychain mode!
